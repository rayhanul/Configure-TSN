Deadline compliance before and during online GCL replacement on a physical TSN testbed. Results aggregate three transitions from RL C9-on to Problem 3 heuristic C9-on across 17 ports on eight switches. Baseline transmitted-packet counts are matched to the activation counts per flow and trial (353,280 per phase). Deadline misses increase from 0 to 25,959 (7.35%); all misses occur on flows 7 and 8, which the replacement schedule does not admit. Skipped sender releases are excluded. This comparison includes an admission-set change and does not isolate activation transients.
